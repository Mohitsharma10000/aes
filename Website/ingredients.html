Clarity

What we favour. What we leave out.

We favour

  • Plant-derived surfactants They lift grease and film while keeping everyday use in mind.
  • Essential oils, used with intent A quiet signature note — not a cloud of synthetic perfume.
  • Thoughtful preservation Products that sit by the sink need a safe system against spoilage.
  • Full, plain-language labels Technical names plus what the ingredient is for.

We leave out

  • Parabens & phthalates Not part of our preservation or fragrance approach.
  • Optical brighteners Visual tricks, not cleaning work.
  • Mystery “fragrance” blends If it is in the bottle, it should be listed.
  • Unnecessary dyes Colour is not a performance claim.

Education

How to read a formula

A skincare brand would never hide its list. Home care should not either. Here is how our kitchen formulas are structured — calmly, without panic.

  1. Surfactants Lift oil, grease, and everyday soil so they can rinse away.
  2. Builders & water helpers Soften water and support clean rinse — useful in hard-water kitchens.
  3. Preservation Keeps formulas stable in a warm, wet kitchen environment.
  4. Fragrance system A single signature botanical family across the range — light, not cloying.
  5. pH adjusters Balance the formula for skin-friendly hand care and surface performance.

Standards

What we will not claim

  • We do not say “chemical-free” or “toxin-free.” Everything is chemistry; the question is which chemistry.
  • We do not invent certifications. Third-party marks appear only when earned and legal-reviewed.
  • We do not use hospital-grade or medical language without proof.
  • We prefer “safer ingredient standards” and full lists over fear-based marketing.
