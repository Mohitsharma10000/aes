General

Quiet rules that always help

  1. Less is usually enough A little product goes further than foam volume suggests. Start small; add if needed.
  2. Rinse and wipe dry Especially on glass and steel — a second dry cloth keeps the finish clear.
  3. Spot-test when unsure Natural stone, unsealed wood, and specialty coatings deserve a small test first.
  4. Store upright, away from heat Keep bottles on the counter or shelf — calm, closed, out of direct flame.
